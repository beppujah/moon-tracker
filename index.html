What studies say

  • Large studies find no link between the full moon and mood, crises or psychiatric visits in the general population (review).
  • Some studies find people fall asleep later and sleep less 3–5 nights before the full moon (Casiraghi 2021); others find nothing (Cordi 2014).
  • Mood cycles tied to the Moon have been reported only in a few people with rapid-cycling bipolar disorder (Wehr 2018).

Each check uses a permutation test with 2,000 shuffles: p is how often a result this strong appears by chance. Because there are six checks, a result counts only when p is below 0.008. With three weeks of data or more, mood is adjusted for the day of the week first. Read results after at least two lunar cycles (about 59 days).

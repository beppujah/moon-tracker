Mood against illumination

r is Pearson's correlation between mood (−2 to +2) and the Moon's illuminated fraction. The p-value comes from a permutation test with 2,000 shuffles: it estimates how often a link this strong would appear by chance. Read results after at least two lunar cycles (about 59 days).
